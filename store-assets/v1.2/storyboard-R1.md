# Mook Worlds 1.2 — Art-directed screenshot campaign R1

Release proof: version 1.2 build 3, commit `ac79d30`, iPhone and iPad portrait, en-US.

## Campaign idea

**Climb into the action.** A tactile arcade expedition where each frame enters a different world. Truthful release UI stays large and readable; atmospheric colour, trails, clouds, rocks and quantum light provide the conversion layer. No repeated generic phone-on-gradient template.

## Frames

### 1 — Climb seven worlds
- Audience/feeling: casual arcade players; discovery and momentum.
- Truthful proof: bottom-to-top world map, winding path, fog, current Mook marker.
- Headline: **Climb seven worlds**
- Support: Seventy handcrafted challenges reveal new rules as you rise.
- Context: sunrise cloud canyon with a vertical route glow continuing beyond the product capture.
- Composition: map capture dominates lower two-thirds; top copy and logo; no generic device shell.
- Distinction: exploration/world overview.

### 2 — Tap. Swipe. Hold. Break.
- Audience/feeling: action players; immediate tactile energy.
- Truthful proof: live eight-cell board with green, blue, yellow, rock and purple mechanics.
- Headline: **Tap. Swipe. Hold. Break.**
- Support: Read the board fast and survive thirty-second challenges.
- Context: kinetic radial trails matching the five mechanic colours, focused around the live board.
- Composition: gameplay crop is largest frame in campaign, slightly tilted with controlled motion lines.
- Distinction: core interaction.

### 3 — Every world changes the rules
- Audience/feeling: players seeking variety; surprise and mastery.
- Truthful proof: Quantum Flux gameplay where cells and arrows can change.
- Headline: **Every world changes the rules**
- Support: Adapt as colours, directions and timing shift beneath you.
- Context: deep indigo space, split cyan/magenta quantum arcs and small star particles.
- Composition: gameplay capture embedded in a luminous portal, headline anchored left/top.
- Distinction: escalating late-game mechanic.

### 4 — Choose your advantage
- Audience/feeling: strategic players; control and preparation.
- Truthful proof: level briefing with staged power unlocks and inventory counts.
- Headline: **Choose your advantage**
- Support: Equip saved powers before the next challenge begins.
- Context: four elemental plinths—sky, sun, rock and time—using only abstract symbols from shipped UI.
- Composition: briefing UI floats over a warm tactical altar field.
- Distinction: customization/preparation; no purchase claim.

### 5 — Endless keeps escalating
- Audience/feeling: score chasers; speed and replayability.
- Truthful proof: Endless Play live run, timer, score, mixed mechanics and checkpoint system.
- Headline: **Endless keeps escalating**
- Support: Bank checkpoints, use one continue and chase your high score.
- Context: infinity tunnel with 30-second colour bands and a two-minute checkpoint ring.
- Composition: UI leans forward into the tunnel; high-contrast score remains readable.
- Distinction: replay/payoff.

## Prohibited

No fabricated characters, rankings, multiplayer, ads, purchase success, impossible scores, fake app chrome, customer data or claims absent from build 3. Store screenshot omitted from the primary campaign because Adam requested gameplay-led art direction.
