Mook Worlds Support

Mook Worlds is an offline portrait arcade game for iPhone.

Quick help

  • Select an unlocked level from the world map.
  • Choose optional power-ups, then tap Start level.
  • Your unlocked levels, best stars, and sound setting are stored locally on your device.
  • Deleting the app removes that locally stored progress.
