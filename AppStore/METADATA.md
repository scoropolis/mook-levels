## Description

Mook Worlds is a fast, tactile journey through seven worlds and seventy handcrafted levels.

Each challenge lasts thirty game seconds. Tap green targets, dodge red traps, swipe arrows, hold sun cells, break rocks, and react to one-second purple flashes. Every world introduces a new rule, then combines it with everything you have learned.

**Seven distinct worlds**

Travel from Green Valley and Sky Current to Reverse Realm and Quantum Flux. Each world contains ten increasingly demanding levels.

**Quick, tactile challenges**

Read the eight-cell board and react with taps, swipes, holds, and timed multi-hit actions.

**Choose your powers**

Equip Sky God, Sun God, Rock God, or Time Slow before a level. Earn power-ups by mastering levels or add optional packs from the Mook Store.

**Endless Play**

Complete World 5 to unlock an escalating run with new mechanics, checkpoint continues, and a saved high score.

**Earn every star**

Finish with lives remaining to earn up to four stars. Your best results, unlocked path, and inventory stay saved on your device.

Optional in-app purchases include consumable power-up packs and a restorable one-time Remove Ads purchase. Consumable power-ups are not restorable. Purchases are not required to complete the game. No account is required.

## App Review notes

Mook Worlds is a portrait arcade game with no account requirement. From the world map, select an available level, optionally equip saved power-ups, and press **Start level**. Complete World 5 to reveal Endless Play.

Tap **Store** on the world map to view the six in-app purchases. The four five-packs add five units of the named power-up. The variety pack adds three of each power-up (12 total). These consumables are stored locally and are not restorable. Remove Ads is a non-consumable entitlement and can be restored with **Restore Remove Ads**.

Interstitial ads are designed to appear only after losses and are capped by time, loss count, and session count. Production ad serving remains disabled until the signed release candidate passes consent, no-fill, frequency-cap, and Remove Ads suppression testing; the final review notes must be reconciled with the uploaded build before submission.
