## Description

**Master every world.**

Mook Worlds turns quick reactions into a 70-level arcade journey. Each world introduces a new rule, then combines everything you have learned into faster, trickier challenges.

- Tap green targets and avoid red traps.
- Swipe blue arrows in the correct direction.
- Hold yellow cells until they shrink away.
- Break numbered rocks before time runs out.
- React to fading purple cells.
- Survive reversed controls and shifting quantum targets.
- Earn up to four stars on every level.
- Equip optional power-ups before each run.
- Keep your progress on your device and play offline.

No account is required. There are no purchases, ads, or social features in version 1.0.

## App Review notes

Mook Worlds is a portrait, offline arcade game with no account requirement. Select Level 1 on the world map, optionally equip power-ups, and press **Start level**. The first target begins the timer. Later worlds introduce swipes, holds, rocks, fading cells, reversed controls, and changing quantum targets.

Version 1.0 contains no advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions, login, analytics, or user-generated content.
