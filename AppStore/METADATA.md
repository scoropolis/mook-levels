## Description

Mook Worlds is a fast, tactile journey through seven worlds and seventy handcrafted levels.

Each challenge lasts thirty game seconds. Tap green targets, dodge red traps, swipe arrows, hold sun cells, break rocks, and react to one-second purple flashes. Every world introduces a new rule, then combines it with everything you have learned.

**Seven distinct worlds**

Travel from Green Valley and Sky Current to Reverse Realm and Quantum Flux. Each world contains ten increasingly demanding levels.

**Quick, tactile challenges**

Read the eight-cell board and react with taps, swipes, holds, and timed multi-hit actions.

**Choose your powers**

Equip Sky God, Sun God, Rock God, or Time Slow before a level. Use your starter inventory or add optional packs from the Mook Store.

**Endless Play**

Earn 100 total best-score stars to unlock an escalating run with new mechanics, checkpoint continues, and a saved high score.

**Earn every star**

Finish with lives remaining to earn up to four stars. Your best results, unlocked path, and inventory stay saved on your device.

Power-ups are earned through flawless four-star victories. No account or in-app purchase is required.

## App Review notes

Mook Worlds is a portrait arcade game with no account requirement. From the opening screen, choose **Mook Worlds** to enter the ascending world map or unlock **Endless Play** by earning 100 total best-score stars. Select an available level, optionally equip saved power-ups, and press **Start level**.

The first flawless four-star victory on a level awards one power-up and reveals it on the result screen. The Store and all in-app purchases are intentionally absent from this release.

Interstitial ads use Google Mobile Ads and appear only after every third loss, with no time delay. Wins never trigger ads. Google UMP handles consent where required, and Privacy Choices appears when applicable.
