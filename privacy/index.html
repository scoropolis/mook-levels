MOOK WORLDS

Privacy Policy

Effective September 4, 2026

What we collect

Mook Worlds does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, best star results, and audio preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to preserve your game progress and is not sent to the developer.

Device access

Mook Worlds does not request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location, or health data.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released.

Support

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Mook Worlds support page.
