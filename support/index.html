MOOK WORLDS

Support

Mook Worlds is a fast level-based reaction game with seven worlds and seventy levels.

Quick help

  • Your progress and best star results are saved locally on your device.
  • Use the music button during play to mute or restore game audio.
  • If local progress is removed with the app, it cannot be recovered from a server.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, level number, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords or other sensitive information.
