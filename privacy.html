Mook Worlds Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Mook Worlds does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Game progress, star results, preferences, and sound settings are stored only on your device.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or user-generated content in version 1.0.

Apple may process App Store downloads and diagnostic information under Apple's own privacy policy. If Mook Worlds adds a service that changes its data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.
