Mook Worlds Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Mook Worlds does not require an account. Game progress, star results, power-up inventory, preferences, and sound settings are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Advertising-enabled versions of Mook Worlds use Google Mobile Ads to provide occasional interstitial ads after gameplay losses. Google and its advertising partners may process an IP address or coarse location, device identifiers, advertising data, ad and product interactions, crash data, performance data, and other diagnostic information for third-party advertising, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service operation. Device identifiers may be used to track activity across apps or services for advertising purposes.

Mook Worlds uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent where required and to provide privacy choices. In the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, ads are not requested until the consent system reports that ads may be requested. A Privacy choices control appears in the app when additional choices are available.

Mook Worlds does not itself sell personal information. Google processes advertising data under its own policies. Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases and App Store services

Apple processes App Store downloads, in-app purchases, payment information, and related diagnostics under Apple's privacy policy. Mook Worlds receives only the purchase result and transaction information needed to deliver or restore eligible in-app content; it does not receive your payment-card details.

Your choices

You can decline or adjust advertising consent where the applicable privacy message offers those choices. You may also use the in-app Privacy choices control when shown. Deleting the app removes locally stored game data from that device.
