Mook Worlds Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Mook Worlds does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. Game progress, star results, preferences, and sound settings are stored only on your device.

The released version 1.0 does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or user-generated content. A future version 1.1 is being developed with Apple in-app purchases and optional Google-served interstitial advertising, but those services are not live in version 1.0.

Apple may process App Store downloads, purchases, and diagnostic information under Apple's own privacy policy. Before any advertising-enabled update is released, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be revised to describe the final Google Mobile Ads configuration, consent choices, territories, and data practices.
